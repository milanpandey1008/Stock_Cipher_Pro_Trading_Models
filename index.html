Institutional Trading Entry Models Guidebook

Aggressive Rocket Entry

Description: Enter immediately after a Rocket signal forms from a major support zone to capture the move at its earliest stage.

Rules:

  • Rocket must form at a Daily, 3-Day, or Weekly Support Zone identified by Buyer's POI and POR.
  • Avoid rocket forming in the middle of the chart with no nearby support.
  • Avoid rocket that form after a Bullish Weak Leg, as they have a higher chance of failure.
  • Check the candlestick chart and avoid entries if the stock has been aggressively pumped within the last 5 minutes.
  • Enter at the Entry Price displayed on the chart.
  • Use the SL and TP displayed on the chart.
Risk Level: High Best For: Early entries from strong support-based reversals.

MPP Zone Entry

Description: The highest-probability entry model. Enter at the MPP Zone for maximum reward with controlled risk.

Rules:

  • The MPP Zone contains 3 entry levels.
  • Divide capital into 3 equal parts and buy at each MPP level.
  • Enter only within the MPP Zone displayed on the chart.
  • Weekly MPP Zones are the strongest, followed by 3-Day MPP Zones, then Daily MPP Zones.
  • Priority: Weekly > 3-Day > Daily.
  • Use the same SL displayed on the chart.
  • Use the same TP levels displayed on the chart.

Trade Management:

  • If NEPSE loses momentum or bearish sentiment increases, exit at the Entry Price shown by Rocket.
  • If market conditions remain favorable, continue targeting the Cipher TP Levels.
  • Hold the position if Aggressive Buyers appear on the chart.
  • Do not exit while aggressive buyers remain active.
  • Exit only when a 👽Devil Icon (Buyer Absorption) appears on the chart.
Risk: Medium Success: Very High Best For: Precision entries.

Follow-Through Entry (FTE)

Description: A trend-continuation setup that allows traders to join an existing move after Aggressive Buyers and a Follow-Through Entry (FTE) signal appear on the Cipher chart.

Rules:

  • Aggressive Buyers must be present on the chart.
  • A valid FTE signal must appear.
  • The setup works only in strong trending markets.
  • The trade is only valid on the next trading day after the FTE signal appears.
  • Check the candlestick chart and avoid entries if the stock has been aggressively pumped within the last 5 minutes.
  • Enter at the FTE Entry Price displayed on the chart.
  • Place SL: Just below the FTE candle low, or below the Rocket Entry Price (only if you enter around the opening price of FTE candle).
  • Use the TP levels displayed on the FTE signal chart

Trade Management:

  • Exit immediately after the shares arrive in your Demat account.
  • If the trade is in significant profit, continue holding.
  • In high-profit situations, stay in the trade until a 👽Devil Icon (Buyer Absorption) appears.
Risk: Medium Success: High Best For: Riding momentum.

Trade Before Sellers Get Trapped (TSGT)

Description: A high-probability reversal setup that anticipates seller exhaustion before a Rocket signal forms.

Rules:

  • Aggressive Sellers must be visible on the Daily or 3-Day timeframe.
  • Price must be entering a Weekly Support Range with POR (Point of Release) and Buyers' POI (Point of Interest).
  • As soon as aggressive sellers enter the Weekly Support Range, begin accumulating.
  • Divide capital into 3 equal parts: Buy at Range High, Mid, and Low.
  • Place SL just below the low of the Weekly Range.

Confluence Factors:

  • Higher probability if supported by a Virgin Bearish Weak Leg on: Weekly or Daily timeframe.
  • Weekly Virgin Bearish Weak Leg carries greater weight.

Take Profit:

  • Wait for a Daily Rocket to form inside the Weekly Range.
  • Follow the TP levels displayed on the Cipher chart.
  • Alternatively, use the Cipher Liquidity Map for profit targets.
Risk: Medium Success: Very High Best For: Catching major reversals.
